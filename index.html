Naan fever nu sonnapo, nee konjam concern kaatuva nu expect pannen. Aana adhu kooda miss aachu. Munnaadi nee enga ponaalum solluva; ippo adhuvum illa. Namma daily enna panrom nu share pannadhu kooda ninnuduchu.

Sometimes nee message pannitu phone-a vechittu thoongura maari irukku. Pesanum nu naan wait pannitu iruppen. Munnaadi nee call pannuva; call history-la un calls irukkum. Ippo calls-um illa, andha time-um illa.

Unakku time illa nu theriyum. Aana munnaadi spend panna time-la irundhu ippo 5 minutes kooda kashtam nu sonnapo romba hurt aachu. Naan replaced aana maari feel panren nu sonna, adhu argument-ah maaridudhu. Naan overthink panreno nu enakkum sila neram doubt varudhu.

Naan videos anuppi, unna miss panren nu explain pannirukken. Aana nee oru thadava kooda namma friendship miss panren nu sollala. Naan enna panren nu nee kekkuradhum illa. Idhellam sollradhu unna blame panna illa; enakku epdi feel aagudhu nu puriya vekka dhaan.

Naan un number delete panniten nu sonnapo, nee casual-ah “okay” nu sonnadhu hurt aachu. Adhu unakku perusa theriyama irundhirukkalam. Aana enakku adhu easy-ah eduthukka mudiyala.

Naan unna force panni pesa vaikka virumbala. Unakku space venumna eduthuko. Aana friendship rendu per effort-um irundha dhaan nalla irukkum. Naan mattum try panra maari feel aaga koodadhu. Enakku namma friendship lifelong irukkanum; strangers aaga vendaam.

Idhu love illa da. Pure friendship. Un friend-ah irundhu namma bond-ah miss panra oruthiyoda message mattum. 🤍
